Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pizza Big Boss mobile game (package com.idc.pizzaboss, the “Game”) handles information when you play it. By installing or using the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We, the developer, do not run our own servers and do not collect, store or sell personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or precise location. The Game does not require an account or sign-in.

Game progress stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (for example cash, upgrades, unlocked items, sound and vibration preferences) are saved locally on your device. This data never leaves your device except through Android’s own backup feature, if you have enabled it in your Google account settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch and occasional interstitial ads, provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may automatically collect:

  • Your device’s Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (ads viewed or tapped)
  • Diagnostic and performance data

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in a region where consent is required, the Game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Game’s settings screen. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized ads.

Opting out of personalized ads

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The Game offers optional in-app purchases such as “Remove Ads”, perks and in-game cash. All payments are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details. We only receive a confirmation of which item was purchased so that it can be delivered in the Game. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Device permissions

  • Vibration – used only for haptic feedback during gameplay. It can be turned off in settings.
  • Internet / network state – required by the advertising and billing services above.

5. Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will work to have it removed.

6. Data sharing and retention

We do not sell or share your personal information. The only third parties that receive data from the Game are Google AdMob and Google Play as described above. Locally stored game data stays on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the Game.

7. Security

Because we do not collect personal data on our own servers, there is no developer-held database that could be exposed. Data handled by Google services is protected under Google’s security practices.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Since we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google; you can also contact us and we will help.

Deleting your data: uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage in Settings → Apps → Pizza Big Boss → Storage, permanently removes all game data from your device.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the Game after changes means you accept the updated policy.
